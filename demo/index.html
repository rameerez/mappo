Land: grid vs vector

Grid contours

Traced from the packed bitmask. Blocky by design, follows cols, costs no extra bytes.

<world-map cols="120"
  land="outline"
  land-stroke="#5f7a4a"></world-map>

Vector coastlines

Real Natural Earth outlines at 1/32°. Smooth at any size, independent of cols. ~13 KB.

<world-map cols="120"
  land="outline"
  land-source="vector"
  land-stroke="#5f7a4a"></world-map>

Filled + coast

One geometry, filled and stroked. Inland seas stay empty — the rings are wound for it.

<world-map land="solid outline"
  land-source="vector"
  land-color="#c9d3bd"
  land-stroke="#5f7a4a"></world-map>

Country borders

Every national boundary, vector only — a raster cannot express a border that follows a river.

<world-map land="solid"
  land-source="vector"
  borders
  borders-color="#5f7a4a"
  borders-opacity="0.75"></world-map>

Globe

Vector globe, leaning

Coastlines clipped to the visible hemisphere — fills close along the horizon, not across it.

<world-map mode="globe"
  land="solid outline"
  land-source="vector"
  roll="-14.3"></world-map>

Everything at once

Vector land, borders, graticule, a real axial tilt, spinning.

<world-map mode="globe" rotate-speed="6"
  tilt="23.44" land="solid outline"
  land-source="vector" borders
  graticule meridians="24" parallels="11">
</world-map>
Lisbon Tokyo

Your DOM, positioned

Children with data-lat/data-lon are placed every frame. Real links, real text.

<world-map mode="globe" land="solid" land-source="vector">
  <a data-lat="38.7" data-lon="-9.1"><span>Lisbon</span></a>
</world-map>

/* --mappo-depth: 1 facing you → 0 at the limb */
span { opacity: calc(.3 + .7*var(--mappo-depth,1)) }

Option reference

land            "dots" | "solid" | "outline" | "solid outline"
land-source     "grid" (bitmask) | "vector" (Natural Earth)
land-color      fill        → falls back to dot-color
land-stroke     coastline   → falls back to land-color
land-stroke-width
borders         country borders (vector)
borders-color / borders-width / borders-opacity
mode            "flat" | "globe"
tilt            axial tilt, degrees (23.44 = Earth)
roll            lean in the screen plane, degrees
rotate-speed    deg/sec (globe)
graticule       meridians / parallels
                graticule-color / equator-color
                graticule-opacity / equator-opacity
cols            grid resolution
lat-min/lat-max framing
overlays        position data-lat/data-lon children
