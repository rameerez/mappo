A port of the Geographic Distribution panel from LicenseSeat's product analytics. mappo draws the land and the country borders and hands out the projection; the fills between them come from demo/countries.js: 205 countries keyed by ISO code, 23 KB gzipped, cut from the same pinned Natural Earth data as mappo's borders with the same simplifier, so they register with them vertex for vertex. The library does not ship that yet. It has country borders as one flat list of anonymous rings, which is enough to draw Brazil's edge and not enough to fill it in.

Hover a country for its number, or hover Other to see where the long tail is. A country too small to have a shape at this scale is a dot on Natural Earth's anchor for it. The original is a Miller projection; this is equirectangular, so the same geography sits a little squatter in the same box. More demos.
